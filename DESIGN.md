## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an affectionate, keepsake-driven digital sanctuary celebrating Vietnamese Women's Day (20/10). Designed specifically for couples honoring shared milestones, memories, and personal appreciation notes, the aesthetic radiates tenderness, calm joy, and gentle nostalgia. 

The visual style embraces **Soft Organic Minimalism with Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous cream and soft warm airiness that feels like a cherished diary page or botanical stationery.
- Softly rounded forms and playful organic pills reminiscent of matcha confectionery and pressed petals.
- Delicate, warm-tinted borders and gentle ambient diffuse glows that evoke hand-crafted paper and filtered morning sunlight.
- Emotional pacing that prioritizes visual intimacy, handwritten-like accents, and respectful typographic treatment for Vietnamese diacritics.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in botanical matcha tones, creamy whipped foams, and subtle blush highlights:

- **Primary (`#4A7C59` - Deep Matcha Sage):** Used for focal headlines, key interactive icons, active states, and grounding CTAs. Delivers clarity, contrast, and calm elegance.
- **Secondary (`#8FCB9B` - Soft Mint Dew):** Accompanies key actions, pill indicators, photo frame borders, and playful celebratory toggles.
- **Tertiary (`#F3C5B6` - Warm Rose / Apricot Cream):** A compassionate accent reserved for romantic emphasis, heart reactions, gift tag badges, and 20/10 celebration markers.
- **Surface & Backgrounds:**
  - Base Canvas: `#FBFBF7` (Alabaster Warm Cream)
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Subtle Surface Tint: `#F5F9F6` (Pale Mint Foam)
  - Surface Border Accent: `#D7EED9` (Pressed Matcha Leaf)
- **Neutrals (`#5A645D`):** Body copy rests in an herbal, charcoal-softened sage rather than harsh black, creating high legibility while maintaining a soft, printed-paper feel.

## Typography

The type system blends structural modern proportions with friendly terminals, specifically optimized for complex Vietnamese diacritical marks (dấu mũ, móc, ngã, hỏi, nặng):

- **Headlines & Labels (`Be Vietnam Pro`):** Engineered natively for Vietnamese typography, rendering tone marks with balanced optical spacing and zero collision. Semi-bold and bold weights bring rhythmic, gentle authority to dates, commemorative titles, and quotes.
- **Body & Story Text (`Nunito Sans`):** Features rounded geometric terminals that complement the welcoming, intimate tone of love letters and photo captions, while maintaining effortless readability across paragraph lengths.
- Vietnamese text renders with open line heights (`1.5` to `1.65`) to give tone accents breathing space.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is centered around an intentional, album-inspired storytelling flow using a soft-centered 12-column grid on desktop and an adaptive fluid single-column spine on mobile:

- **Desktop (min-width: 1024px):** 12-column layout maxed at 1120px container width, with `3.5rem` outer margins and `2rem` gutters. Elements flow in balanced masonry cards, scrapbook photo duos, and letter spreads.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margins, allowing memories to tile cleanly in paired configurations.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer canvas padding. Interactive timeline elements align to a subtle central vertical dotted axis (`#D7EED9`).
- **Rhythm:** Generous vertical sections (`space-xl`) grant reflective moments between milestone chapters, avoiding information density and emphasizing quiet emotional value.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Cream Layers & Ambient Meadow Shadows** rather than stark drops:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Smooth `#FBFBF7` matte paper base.
- **Level 1 (Memory Cards, Notes):** Surface `#FFFFFF` bordered with a 1.5px continuous outline in `#D7EED9`, supported by an ultra-diffused shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(74, 124, 89, 0.07)`.
- **Level 2 (Popups, Floating Audio Player, Milestone Modals):** Pure white container with `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(74, 124, 89, 0.12)`, imparting a floating floral token appearance.
- **Glass Accents:** Translucent header navigation bars and floating photo tooltips apply `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(245, 249, 246, 0.85)` with a delicate inner hair-border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`).

## Shapes

The design system uses pill-shaped, organic silhouettes to express gentleness and warmth:

- Standard controls, chips, message pills, and interactive buttons adopt full pill radii (`9999px` or `roundedness: 3`).
- Memory image frames, journal cards, and milestone dialogs use `rounded-xl` (3rem / 48px on large elements, 2rem / 32px on mid-sized elements).
- Photo placeholders emulate soft-curved instax sheets with slightly exaggerated bottom borders and organically rounded picture windows.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped, background `#4A7C59`, text `#FFFFFF`, with `0.75rem 1.75rem` padding. On hover, shifts to `#3F6B4C` with a soft mint halo (`0 0 0 4px #D7EED9`).
- **Secondary / Romantic Button:** Pale mint background `#F5F9F6`, border `1.5px solid #8FCB9B`, text `#4A7C59`.
- **Tertiary Love Action:** Warm blush `#F3C5B6` background, text `#4A7C59`, applied to heart counters and special 20/10 gift unveils.

### Memory Cards & Photo Frames
- Crafted with pure white card surfaces, `2rem` rounded corners, and a 1.5px border of `#D7EED9`.
- Features an optional top tape or pin motif in `#F3C5B6` (Warm Rose) for an analog scrapbooking atmosphere.
- Photos maintain an internal soft radius of `1.25rem`.

### Chips & Milestone Badges
- Interactive date filters ("Năm nhất", "Kỷ niệm đầu", "20/10/2024") utilize pill shapes with `#F5F9F6` background, `#4A7C59` typography, and `#8FCB9B` borders on active states.

### Audio & Memory Player
- A floating capsule pill at the viewport base in frosted `#F5F9F6` (blur: 16px), containing audio progress in `#8FCB9B`, matcha play/pause toggles, and track labels in `label-sm`.

### Form Fields & Secret Note Inputs
- Inputs adopt `1.5rem` rounded contours, `#FFFFFF` fill, `#D7EED9` idle stroke, and `#4A7C59` focused rings.
- Placeholder text in muted neutral `#8A968D` providing tender writing prompts (e.g., "Gửi lời chúc 20/10 tới em...").

### Checkboxes & Love Wishlist Toggles
- Custom circular checkmarks filled with `#8FCB9B` and a white heart or check icon upon selection, accompanied by smooth scaling micro-interactions.
